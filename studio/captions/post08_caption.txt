Is that really maple syrup? 🍁🥞

Most "pancake syrup" isn't. Flip the bottle and read the ingredients:

✅ Pure maple syrup: one ingredient. Maple sap, boiled down.
❌ A typical pancake syrup: corn syrup, high fructose corn syrup, water, cellulose gum, caramel color, salt, natural and artificial flavor, preservatives. Maple isn't even on the list.

Words that mean real maple syrup:
• "Pure maple syrup" or "100% pure maple syrup"
• A grade with a color and taste, like "Grade A Amber Color and Rich Taste"

Words that mean it isn't pure maple syrup:
• Pancake syrup, table syrup or breakfast syrup
• Maple-flavored or artificially flavored

The rule: in the US, maple syrup must be made from maple sap and be at least 66% sugar, with nothing sweet added (FDA standard, 21 CFR 168.140).

Why real syrup costs more: it takes about 40 gallons of sap and hours of boiling to make one gallon, all in a short season each spring.
