MAPLE SCAVENGER HUNT 🍁🔍 A family game for this weekend. Save this post and head outside!

Can you find...
☐ A maple leaf with smooth edges (could be a sugar maple, the sweetest!)
☐ A maple leaf with saw-tooth edges (red or silver maple)
☐ A maple "helicopter" seed (sugar maples drop theirs in fall)
☐ Twigs with buds in pairs (maple buds grow opposite each other)
☐ A maple about 38 inches around (that's 12 inches across, big enough to tap!)
☐ The biggest maple on your street

🖍️ Bonus: make a leaf rubbing. Lay a leaf bumpy side up, cover it with paper and rub with the long side of a peeled crayon. Write down which tree it came from.

🎀 Then mark your maples with a ribbon. When the leaves are gone this winter, you'll know exactly which trees to tap.

Share your finds and tag @mapletapper. We'd love to see them! 👇
