The perfect tapping spot might be on your drive home. 🍁

Jim pulled over to film a row of big sugar maples that a farmer planted along a country road decades ago. Trees lined up near a road make sap runs easy: no trekking through the woods, just pull up and empty the buckets.

Before you tap a tree like this:
✅ Ask first. Trees along the street often belong to the town or village, not the homeowner. (Jim's idea: pay them in syrup!)
✅ Tap only healthy trees at least 12 inches across, measured 4½ feet up.
✅ Road salt is hard on sugar maples, so pick trees with full, healthy crowns.
✅ Park well off the road and watch for traffic.

Scout your trees now while the leaves are still on, then tap in late winter.

New to tapping? Start at mapletapper.com (link in bio).
