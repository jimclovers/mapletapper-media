=== 05 Maple Pumpkin Pie (Tue Nov 17, noon) ===
Maple Kitchen No. 05: Maple Pumpkin Pie 🥧🍁 Save this for Thanksgiving!

The classic pumpkin pie, sweetened with real maple syrup. Smooth, warmly spiced and just a little caramel-y.

🛒 What you need: a pie crust, canned pumpkin, maple syrup, eggs, cream and pumpkin pie spice
⏱ How long: about 1 hour, plus cooling
🧒 Kids can help: whisking the filling and pouring it into the crust
💡 Make it a day ahead. It slices best after a night in the fridge.

=== 06 Maple Pecan Pie (Thu Nov 19, noon) ===
Maple Kitchen No. 06: Maple Pecan Pie 🥧🍁 No corn syrup needed.

All the gooey, nutty goodness you love, with pure maple syrup doing the sweetening.

🛒 What you need: a pie crust, maple syrup, brown sugar, eggs, butter, a little flour, vanilla and pecans
⏱ How long: about 1 hour, plus cooling
🧒 Kids can help: arranging the pecans in the crust
💡 Let it cool at least 2 hours so the filling sets.

=== 07 Maple Whipped Cream (Sat Nov 21, noon) ===
Maple Kitchen No. 07: Maple Whipped Cream ☁️🍁

The easiest upgrade for your Thanksgiving pies: fluffy whipped cream sweetened with maple syrup. Three ingredients, five minutes.

🛒 What you need: cold heavy cream, maple syrup and a little vanilla
⏱ How long: 5 minutes
🧒 Kids can help: taking turns with the whisk (by hand takes about 5 minutes!)
💡 Chill the bowl first. Cold cream whips faster.

=== 08 Maple-Roasted Sweet Potatoes (Sun Nov 22, noon) ===
Maple Kitchen No. 08: Maple-Roasted Sweet Potatoes 🍠🍁

Crispy-edged sweet potato cubes with a light maple glaze. A simple side for Thanksgiving, or any night of the week.

🛒 What you need: sweet potatoes, oil or butter, maple syrup, salt and a pinch of cinnamon
⏱ How long: 35 minutes for 4 to 6 servings
🧒 Kids can help: tossing the cubes in oil and spreading them on the pan
💡 Add the syrup near the end so it doesn't scorch.
