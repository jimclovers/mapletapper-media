Before you bag the leaves… play leaf detective! 🔍🍂

Jump in first, of course. Then:
1️⃣ Grab a leaf and check the edges. Smooth edges could mean sugar maple, the top syrup tree. Saw-tooth edges mean red or silver maple (tappable, just less sweet).
2️⃣ Snap a fresh leaf stem from the tree. A milky drop means Norway maple, a common look-alike.
3️⃣ Found a sugar maple? Look up, find its tree and tie on a ribbon. Once the leaves drop, maples are much harder to tell apart, and you'll want to know which tree to tap in late winter.
