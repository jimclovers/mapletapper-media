=== 03 Maple Pecan Granola (Sat Nov 14, noon) ===
Maple Kitchen No. 03: Maple Pecan Granola 🍁🥣

Crunchy oat and pecan clusters baked in maple syrup. Make a jar this weekend and breakfast is done all week.

🛒 What you need: oats, pecans, maple syrup, butter or oil, cinnamon, salt and raisins
⏱ How long: about 45 minutes for 5 cups
👧 Kids can help: measuring the oats and stirring until every oat is shiny
✨ The secret: let it cool on the pan without stirring for big clusters

=== 02 Maple Hot Cocoa (Thu Nov 12, noon) ===
Maple Kitchen No. 02: Maple Hot Cocoa ☕🍁

Cold afternoon? This one's for the kids. Rich, creamy cocoa sweetened with real maple syrup instead of white sugar.

🛒 What you need: milk, cocoa powder, maple syrup, vanilla and a pinch of salt
⏱ How long: 10 minutes for 2 mugs
🧒 Kids can help: measuring the cocoa and whisking it into a smooth paste
⚠️ Grown-ups handle the hot pot, and let it cool a bit before little ones sip

=== 04 Maple Sap Tea (Sun Nov 15, noon) ===
Maple Kitchen No. 04: Maple Sap Tea 🍁🫖 Save this one for sap season!

When the sap runs in late winter, brew your tea with it instead of water. It's lightly sweet with just a hint of maple.

🛒 What you need: fresh maple sap, tea bags and a coffee filter
⏱ How long: 10 minutes for 2 cups
🧊 Sap safety: sap spoils like milk. Keep it below 40°F, use it within a day or two, and always bring it to a rolling boil before drinking.
