KIDS CORNER: Is your tree big enough to tap? 🌳📏

Foresters measure trees the same way every time, and kids can too. All you need is a string and a ruler (or a tape measure).

1️⃣ Find 4½ feet up the trunk. That's "breast height," about a grown-up's chest.
2️⃣ Wrap a string around the trunk there. Keep it level and snug, and pinch where the ends meet.
3️⃣ Lay the string on a ruler. That's how far around the tree is.
4️⃣ Divide by 3.14 to find how far across it is.

🍁 Our size guide:
• Under 12 inches across (under about 38 inches around): too small. Let it grow!
• 12 to 18 inches across (about 38 to 57 inches around): big enough for 1 tap
• Over 18 inches across: a big tree that can take more than one tap

Why 3.14? It's pi! Every circle is a little more than 3 times as far around as it is across, and a tree trunk is close to a circle.

Tip: if there's a bump or branch at 4½ feet, measure just above or below it.
