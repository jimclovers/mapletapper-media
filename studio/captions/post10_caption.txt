Bake with maple instead of sugar 🍁🧁 Save this for holiday baking!

The 4-step swap:
1️⃣ Use ¾ cup maple syrup for every 1 cup of white sugar.
2️⃣ Cut other liquids by 2 to 4 tablespoons for each cup of syrup.
3️⃣ Add ¼ to ½ teaspoon baking soda, unless the recipe already has buttermilk, sour milk or sour cream.
4️⃣ Lower the oven 25°F. Maple browns faster at the edges.

Quick chart (sugar → maple syrup):
• 2 cups → 1½ cups
• 1 cup → ¾ cup
• ½ cup → ¼ cup + 2 Tbsp
• ¼ cup → 3 Tbsp

Flavor tip: want more maple flavor? Use a darker syrup. Dark and very dark grades taste the strongest.

Holiday ideas from our Guide to Maple Tapping: maple mashed sweet potatoes, maple bacon Brussels sprouts, maple caramel sauce and maple granola.

Swap tips from University of Maine Extension and the Vermont Maple Sugar Makers' Association.
