Sugar maple or not? Here's Jim's quick field test. 🍁

🌳 Sugar maple: rounded crown, yellow-orange to bronze in fall (sometimes red), and leaves with smooth edges.
🍁 Red and silver maples: saw-tooth edges all around the leaf. They grow fast, so newer neighborhoods plant lots of them.

Can you tap them? Yes! Their sap is just less sweet, so you boil longer. Sap needed for 1 gallon of syrup, roughly:
• Sugar maple: 40 gallons
• Red maple: 50 gallons
• Silver maple: 57 gallons

Bonus tip: many maples in newer neighborhoods are red × silver hybrids called Freeman maples (Autumn Blaze is a popular one). Their leaves have deep cuts and saw-tooth edges, so they're not sugar maples either.
