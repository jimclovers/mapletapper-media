Maple KitchenNo. 07 · Method

Let’s cook

1Chill the bowl and beaters in the freezer for 10 minutes. Cold cream whips faster.
2Beat the cream on medium-high until it starts to thicken.
3Drizzle in the maple syrup and vanilla and keep beating until soft peaks form.
4Stop as soon as it holds its shape. Keep it in the fridge until serving.
Cook’s note: Kids can take turns with a whisk. Whipping by hand takes about 5 minutes!
